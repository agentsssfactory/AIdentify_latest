Terms of Service

AIdentify is a workspace of AI agents for a business you name at signup. You pick the industry. Digital marketing is the first pack. Other packs, including clinics, stay available.

Your account

You can start on $1 of platform model use or bring your own AI key. You are responsible for the people you add and for approving anything that would be sent.

Plans and payment

Self-hosted is ₹2,999 a month plus a one-time setup of ₹9,999, waived on an annual plan. Hosted is ₹4,999 a month. Invoices are paid through Razorpay, including UPI as a Razorpay method, when test keys are configured. AIdentify does not publish a personal UPI id for plan payment.

WhatsApp

QR linking is unofficial. WhatsApp may restrict the number. Outbound customer messages need your approval. The Meta or Gupshup API is an optional upgrade.

Customer data

Records stay in this system. We do not offer a Salesforce or other external CRM sync. You can export a CSV of your contacts at any time.

Acceptable use

Do not use the product to send unsolicited bulk messages, to break the law, or to store data you have no right to hold.
