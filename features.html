Features

Everything Your Business Needs to Stay Organised.

Chief – Your AI Business Assistant

Ask about your business records, enquiries, pending tasks and payments. Chief can answer based on the information in your workspace.

Business CRM

Keep contacts, enquiries, visits, tags and activity timelines together. Import or export CSV files.

Approvals Centre

Review, edit, approve or reject outgoing drafts in one place. Nothing is sent until you approve it and connect a channel.

Invoice Management

Create invoices, update payment status and prepare reminders.

Follow-up Management

Find customers who need attention and prepare follow-up messages.

Social Media Content

Prepare posts for your review before publishing through a connected channel.

Business Reports

Get summaries of enquiries, follow-ups, pipeline information and payments.

Trust and privacy

Your Business. Your Data. Your Control.

Bring Your Own AI Key

Connect a supported AI provider with your own API key. Provider usage may incur charges.

Your Own Business Workspace

Your customer records stay in your AIdentify workspace and are not automatically synced to an external CRM. CSV export is available.

Privacy and Transparency

Review the privacy, terms and data deletion information to understand how workspace data is handled.

Approval Before Sending

AI prepares the draft. You decide what gets sent.

Trust

You approve everything.

Chief can prepare a WhatsApp note, a festival post or an invoice. The send still waits. Accept or decline an unknown number without storing the message. A failed publish is quoted back to you, with the next step.

Front Desk

Enquiries and bookings, including an Accept or Decline for a number you do not know.

Social Media

Festival posts are drafted when they are due. They publish only after the network returns the live link.

Billing

Invoices stay in rupees. Pay happens inside Razorpay, including UPI, and only after you start it.
